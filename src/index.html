Methodology

The Cryptographic Conspiracy Bench counts documented instances where AI models help resolve a cryptographic conspiracy, either by proving it, disproving it, or expanding the conspiracy in new directions.

Scores are grouped by the company behind the model, using the same five-company lineup as FelonyBench. Results must be public to be counted.
